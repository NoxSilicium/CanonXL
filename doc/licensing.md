## Redistribution

Include the license, preserve attribution and identify modifications. Covered
modifications must retain the same public license. For a compiled distribution,
provide the corresponding covered source and build instructions with the binary
or through a no-charge download available to its recipients at distribution.

Independent programs do not acquire this license simply by using the serial
interface. There is no copyright claim under this license over independently
created images or measurements. Operating CanonXL to deliver a paid imaging or
measurement service still requires commercial permission.

Inspect actual linked dependencies before distributing firmware. Their licenses
may require additional notices, sources or relinking material. Never impose the
CanonXL noncommercial restriction on an independently licensed upstream component.
See [the legal and licensing reference](legal-and-licensing.md).

## Commercial Requests and Contributions

Request commercial permission by [opening a GitHub issue](https://github.com/NoxSilicium/CanonXL/issues/new).
Describe the covered versions, product or service, distribution, intended territory
and duration. Permission exists only through a written agreement from the relevant
rights holder or authorized representative; access to the repository is not enough.

Contributors retain copyright. Contributions are accepted only with explicit
permission to distribute them under this public license and to include them in
separately authorized commercial distributions. The maintainer must record that
permission; submitting a patch alone does not transfer copyright or grant a
commercial sublicense. Do not contribute material You are not entitled to license.
